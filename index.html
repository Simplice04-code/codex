GET THE BIG PICTURE
BREADTH-FIRST SEARCH BFS

Explore wide, then go deeper.

Visit every neighbor before moving to the next level. A queue keeps the earliest discoveries first.

Good for finding the shortest path in an unweighted graph.
DEPTH-FIRST SEARCH DFS

Follow your curiosity. Then backtrack.

Follow one branch as far as it goes, then try another. A stack puts the latest discovery on top.

Good for exploring possibilities and checking connectivity.
